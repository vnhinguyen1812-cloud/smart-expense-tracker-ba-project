# Smart Expense Tracker - Project Overview

## Project Objective

Help users manage personal finances more effectively by tracking transactions, monitoring spending behavior, and controlling monthly budgets through automated budget alerts.

---

## Problem Statement

Many banking users struggle to understand their spending patterns and frequently exceed monthly budgets due to limited financial visibility and lack of timely spending alerts.

This leads to:
- poor budgeting habits
- increased financial stress
- low engagement with personal finance tools

---

## Proposed Solution

Develop a Smart Expense Tracker system that automatically categorizes transactions, tracks monthly spending, and provides real-time budget alerts when users approach predefined budget limits.

---

## Target Users

- individual banking customers
- young professionals
- university students
- mobile banking users

---

## Expected Business Impact

- improve financial awareness
- reduce overspending
- increase engagement with fintech tools
- enhance digital banking experience
